# Corpus card: WorkLang corpus v0 (merged, frozen)

This corpus is 100 percent synthetic, generated by TechAura's own simulators, contains no real customer data.

Corpus v0 is the frozen merge of corpus source A (QualBench runs through the trace emitter) and corpus source B (LeadPipe demo lifecycle synthesis plus the demo walkthrough anchor family). Each source froze its own group-aware 80/10/10 split under its own card (corpus/source-a/ and corpus/source-b/); this merge unions the split files and verifies that no group id appears in more than one merged split and that every trace appears exactly once.

## Channels

Source A carries channel "unknown" on every event: QualBench scenarios declare no channel and none was invented. Source B carries each LeadPipe lead's real source as the channel, except walk_in and other, which have no channel bucket and degrade to "unknown" (see the conflicts section).

## Known conflicts and emitter decisions

From source A: QualBench agents report two stage strings with no token in the stage vocabulary, HANDOFF and UNQUALIFIED. Neither is silently mapped into the stage stream: HANDOFF surfaces as handoff events, and a run whose final stage is UNQUALIFIED closes with outcome "lost". From source B: DESIGN.md enumerates the channel buckets as the seven LeadPipe source values, but the Source enum has nine and the demo seed uses walk_in and other, which degrade to "unknown". Both are schema v2 questions, recorded in DESIGN.md.
