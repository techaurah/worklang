## Lifecycle walks

Each seed lead contributes 8 walk(s): 96 lifecycle traces. A trace opens with the recorded beginning (capture, the seed's score when it has one, then stage events along a shortest legal TRANSITIONS path from NEW to the lead's seeded stage, preferring paths that avoid BOOKED so no unrecorded booking is implied), then a seeded random walk over TRANSITIONS from the seeded stage, at most 8 future transitions. The seeded next_action's follow_up fires when it comes due: at the start of the future segment when already due, after the first future transition otherwise. Entering BOOKED emits a booking event. After each future transition the lead stays engaged with the declared probability for its band (hot 85% | warm 75% | nurture 60% | low 45% | none 60%); these are generator parameters, not measured behaviour. Outcomes are end-state: WON or LOST when the walk arrives there, booked when the walk stands at BOOKED at the end, stalled when contact ceases with the seeded next_action still unactioned or the walk is truncated unresolved, dropped when contact ceases with nothing pending.

## Anchor family

13 anchor traces from one execution of the demo walkthrough sequence (seed, then its nine steps, approval mode) against the fake store seeded at the fixed moment LeadPipe's own tests use, Monday 2026-11-23 09:00 UTC; the walkthrough script itself pins no seed moment. The 12 seeded leads' traces are built from their seeded audit entries only (capture, the one deduplicated capture, score); no stage transitions are recorded in the audit log and none are invented, and each closes with the outcome its post-run state names (stage WON, LOST, or BOOKED, else stalled while a next_action is pending, else dropped). The walkthrough lead's trace is recorded dispatch by dispatch, reads included, and closes booked. Note that in LeadPipe a booking does not move the stage, so booking events and stage events are separate signals in this source.

## Known conflicts and emitter decisions

DESIGN.md enumerates the channel bucket set as the seven source values in LeadPipe's demo seed and Source enum. The Source enum in fact has nine values, and the demo seed itself uses two with no channel bucket: walk_in (Grace Liu) and other (Noah Smith). The schema cannot hold them, so both degrade to the channel stream's absence value "unknown" in this corpus; the degradation is a documented emitter decision, and whether they deserve channel tokens is a schema v2 question, recorded in DESIGN.md.
