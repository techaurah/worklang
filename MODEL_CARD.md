# Model card: worklang-mini

worklang-mini clears the bar set in build step 4. On the held-out test
split of corpus v0 it beats the order-2 n-gram baseline on both gated
metrics: family accuracy 0.8511 against 0.7004, and full-composite
exact match 0.2537 against 0.2059. Per the rule in DESIGN.md, the mini
therefore ships as a model claim for corpus v0. Every number on this
card comes from results/mini-v0.json, written by
scripts/train_mini.py from the run it records; no number here is
hand-written.

## Architecture

Decoder-only transformer over the flat WorkLang token stream (BOS, one
vertical token, then 7 tokens per event, EOS):

- d_model 256, 6 layers, 8 heads, context 256 events (1792 tokens).
- Token embedding sized to the reserved vocabulary space (128 rows;
  69 tokens are assigned).
- Prediction order within a position is coarse to fine: a family head
  predicts the event family first, then one head per attribute stream,
  each conditioned on the family through its token embedding.
- Loss is the sum of the per-stream cross-entropies. No masked
  modelling, no auxiliary heads in v0.

## Parameters

5,246,523 parameters, counted from the built module by the training
script. DESIGN.md estimated 5 to 6M before the code existed; the real
count lands inside that estimate.

## Training data

Corpus v0, frozen merge, described in corpus/v0/CORPUS_CARD.md: 409
traces, 4,637 events, 33,686 tokens, from the QualBench emitter
(source A) and the LeadPipe lifecycle synthesiser plus walkthrough
anchors (source B). The model trained on the train split only (325
traces) and was scored on the test split only (46 traces, 544 scored
positions, the step 4 harness protocol). The corpus is 100 percent
synthetic, generated by TechAura's own simulators, and contains no
real customer data.

## Training configuration actually used

- Device: CPU only (PyTorch 2.15.0+cpu), 4 threads, on this VM.
- Epochs 48, learning rate 0.003 with cosine decay, batch size 32,
  weight decay 0.01, gradient clipping at 1.0, seed 20261104.
- Loss fell from 11.7235 (first epoch) to 1.8095 (final epoch).
- Wall time per full run (train, score, calibrate): 2758.6 seconds
  for run 1 and 2415.8 seconds for run 2.

## Evaluation against the baselines

Held-out next-event accuracy on the test split, 544 positions. All
three systems were scored by the same harness on the same positions.

| Stream | worklang-mini | N-gram order-2 | Majority class |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| family | 0.8511 | 0.7004 | 0.2794 |
| channel | 0.9154 | 0.8051 | 0.7537 |
| score_band | 0.9522 | 0.7757 | 0.3732 |
| latency_band | 0.3438 | 0.2684 | 0.2868 |
| stage | 0.8070 | 0.7463 | 0.4320 |
| tool | 0.8713 | 0.7518 | 0.6710 |
| outcome_class | 0.8952 | 0.9430 | 0.9154 |
| full-composite exact match | 0.2537 (138 of 544) | 0.2059 (112 of 544) | 0.0386 (21 of 544) |

The mini beats the n-gram on six of seven streams. The exception is
outcome_class, where the n-gram (0.9430) and even the majority
constant (0.9154) sit above the mini (0.8952): outcome tokens are
mostly "none" at non-terminal positions, and the constant predictors
exploit that base rate more ruthlessly than the mini does.

## Futures calibration

Protocol: one prefix per test trace (all events except the terminal
outcome event), 32 futures sampled per prefix at temperature 1.0,
capped at 64 new events, pooled per prefix group (7 groups in the
test split); total variation distance between the sampled outcome
distribution and the group's empirical test outcomes, over the five
real outcome classes.

- Mean group total variation: 0.2331.
- Pooled total variation: 0.2262.
- 1,472 futures sampled, all 1,472 completed with an outcome event,
  0 truncated.
- Pooled sampled outcomes: booked 706 (48.0 percent), lost 427
  (29.0 percent), stalled 282 (19.2 percent), won 29 (2.0 percent),
  dropped 28 (1.9 percent).
- Pooled empirical outcomes (46 test traces): booked 17
  (37.0 percent), stalled 14 (30.4 percent), lost 8 (17.4 percent),
  dropped 4 (8.7 percent), won 3 (6.5 percent).

Read plainly: the mini over-samples booked and lost relative to the
held-out traces and under-samples stalled, dropped, and won. A total
variation distance of 0.23 is a measured gap, not a calibrated
futures surface; the demo in build step 6 must show its sampled
shares as model behaviour, which is exactly the caveat DESIGN.md
already requires on the page.

## Reproducibility

The training script ran the whole train, score, and calibrate
sequence twice with the same seed. The two runs agree exactly: the
largest absolute delta across all twelve compared metrics (family,
composite, every stream accuracy, both calibration distances, and
final epoch loss) is 0.0.

## Limitations

- Synthetic-only corpus: the mini learns the habits of TechAura's
  own simulators (QualBench agents and the LeadPipe demo lifecycle),
  not real business behaviour. Scores on this page describe fit to
  those simulators on held-out traces from the same generators.
- Latency is declared, not measured, in corpus v0: the latency values
  were assigned by the corpus generators per vertical, never observed
  from a real system. latency_band is the weakest stream for every
  system on the table (mini 0.3438), and no latency claim should be
  made from this model.
- Small corpus, small test split: 46 test traces and 544 positions.
  Differences of a few points are within what a split this size can
  resolve.
- outcome_class accuracy trails both baselines, as noted above.
- Calibration is imperfect (pooled TV 0.2262): sampled futures skew
  toward booked and lost outcomes compared with held-out traces.
