The two prefixes

Sampled outcome distributions, side by side

Each panel aggregates the futures sampled from its prefix. A future runs one sampled event at a time until it reaches its outcome event or the generation cap. Shares are percentages of completed sampled futures; futures that hit the cap without an outcome are counted as truncated and excluded from the shares.

Three sampled trajectories per prefix, decoded back into plain event lines. The prefix events come first; everything after them is one sampled future, exactly as the model produced it.
